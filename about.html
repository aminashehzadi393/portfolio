About

Sites that get found,

I’m an SEO Expert and Web Developer. My work sits at the intersection of marketing and engineering: I audit real business websites, find exactly what keeps them off page one, and fix it — on-page structure, technical crawlability, speed, and the authority signals that decide competitive keywords.

Because I build websites too, I don’t hand you a PDF of problems and walk away. I implement the fixes directly — schema, Core Web Vitals, semantic HTML, internal linking — and my own portfolio scores a perfect 100/100 on Google PageSpeed SEO. I also helped build SEO_skp, a full audit platform that scores any site on SEO, AEO and GEO — the same discipline I apply to client work.

The next frontier: GEO and AEO — making businesses visible not just in Google, but in the AI answers customers increasingly trust. I optimize for where search is going, not where it was.
