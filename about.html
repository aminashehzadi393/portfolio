About

From websites that rank,
to models that reason.

I started in web development and SEO — building fast, search-optimized websites and ranking real client sites. Shipping production work taught me how the web actually works: performance, semantics, and what makes machines understand content.

That curiosity pulled me deeper: if I can make machines find content, can I make them understand it? Today I’m specializing in LLM engineering & model training — currently deep-diving into Python, PyTorch, fine-tuning, RAG pipelines and AI agents, with the whole roadmap tracked in the open on GitHub.

My edge: most AI engineers can’t ship a website, and most web developers can’t train a model. I do both — which means I can take an intelligent system from prototype to a production experience people actually use.
